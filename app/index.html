    How DealHound checks a deal

    1. Find. Deals pulled live from the CheapShark deals API across six stores: Steam, Epic Games Store, GOG, Fanatical, GreenManGaming and Gamesplanet.
    2. Verify. Every game's cheapest price ever was read back from CheapShark, and every Steam price was cross-checked against the Steam store's own API.
    3. Judge. A deal is an all-time low only when its price matches the cheapest ever recorded. Within 15% of it is near all-time low. A deep cut that has still sold cheaper is a huge deal, not a record. A 60%-off price with a far lower cheapest ever is only a strong deal.
    4. Show the work. Each card names its store, its usual price, its sale price, what you save, the cheapest ever, and when the price was read — so you can check it yourself.

    Sources: CheapShark API (deals, cheapest price ever, per-store prices) and the Steam store API (prices and genres), read 27 November 2026, 13:42–13:45 WIB.
